Research in econometrics, causal inference, and applied microeconomics.

I am a Professor of Economics at CREST and ENSAE Paris, where I have been based since 2011. I received my PhD in Economics from Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne in 2009.

My main research interests are in econometric theory, especially treatment effect estimation, partial identification, and inference. I also work on applied questions in industrial organization and labor economics.

I have been Managing Editor of The Review of Economic Studies since 2023.
